## Обраний напрямок: Тиха клінічна поезія

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial wellness design with references to Scandinavian interiors, Swiss typographic discipline, and soft brutalist composition. The layout favors offset columns, visual breathing room, and a calm reading rhythm over a conventional centered marketing grid.

### Core Principles
1. Medical confidence without visual coldness: information is precise, but surfaces are warm and tactile.
2. Asymmetry with purpose: oversized editorial statements, offset cards, and vertical rules create a memorable rhythm.
3. Pastels as atmosphere, not decoration: blush, cloud blue, and sage support trust and calm rather than becoming candy-colored accents.
4. Real utility is visible: prices, booking, contacts, and directions remain easy to find at every important decision point.

### Color Philosophy
The palette is built on warm milk (#FBF8F1), inked olive-black (#1F2924), faded blush (#EACAC8), mist blue (#DCEAF0), and quiet sage (#DCE6D2). Coral red (#C85C52) is reserved for the brand mark and decisive actions, making it feel ownable and human without overwhelming the page.

### Layout Paradigm
An editorial scroll with a fixed utility rail on desktop and a compact mobile navigation. Sections alternate between wide photographic fields and offset text columns. The price list uses a two-column index and accordion-like category rows rather than a dense table.

### Signature Elements
1. Thin coral vertical rules that act as section markers and connect headlines to actions.
2. Soft paper panels with irregular corner radii and subtle grain, paired with fine olive borders.
3. Small handwritten-style micro-labels and oversized serif numerals for time, care, and trust moments.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction should reduce uncertainty. Buttons state their purpose plainly, phone and messaging actions are always one tap away, and the booking form confirms the user’s intent in the same calm tone as the rest of the site. Hover states should feel like a page being gently lifted, not a loud animation.

### Animation
Use 180–280ms transitions with a strong ease-out. On load, editorial blocks reveal with a small upward translation and opacity shift, staggered by 60ms. Cards lift by 4px on hover and photos scale only 1.02. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and never animate layout dimensions.

### Typography System
Display: Cormorant Garamond, 500–600, used for hero statements, section numerals, and short poetic emphasis. Body: Manrope, 400–700, used for navigation, descriptions, prices, and form labels. Eyebrows use Manrope 700 with generous tracking and uppercase styling. H1 is clamp(3.6rem, 8vw, 7.8rem), tight line-height, max three lines.

### Brand Essence
A calm, modern dental clinic in Mykolaiv for people who want precise care delivered with genuine human attention. Different because the clinic communicates medicine with warmth, clarity, and taste.

Personality: attentive, assured, gentle.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are short, specific, and quietly confident. CTAs are verbs with a clear next step. Microcopy reassures without overpromising.

Example lines: “Турбота, яку видно в деталях.” / “Обрати час для консультації”.

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the provided red stylized lion mark as the recognizable anchor, paired with a custom-feeling two-line wordmark: “Актив” in the display serif and “Медікал” in compact Manrope with a coral middle dot. Never render the logo as a generic single-font lockup.

### Signature Brand Color
Coral red #C85C52 — a quiet, warm red that connects the existing lion mark to the human warmth of the clinic.
