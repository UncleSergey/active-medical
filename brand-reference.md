# Brand reference: active-medical.ua

Source reviewed: https://www.active-medical.ua/

## Observed brand system
- Official wordmark uses red accent for Active and a red medical emblem; the word Medical appears in a dark neutral.
- Header is predominantly white/light with a compact logo, thin uppercase navigation, utility phone/contact actions, and a strong red accent used for emphasis.
- Official site uses a broad, information-dense medical-centre architecture: departments, doctors, promotions, news, appointments, contacts and social links.
- The dental site should preserve its cleaner editorial experience while adopting the parent brand's recognizable red/dark/white identity and medical seriousness.
- Existing official contact content includes Active Medical locations in Mykolaiv and a primary phone number, but the dental site must keep its already approved dental-specific contacts and opening hours unless the owner explicitly changes them.

## Design direction for dental site
- Primary: Active Medical red accent.
- Neutrals: warm white, charcoal/near-black, soft gray.
- Secondary: restrained pale blue/pink only as supporting surfaces, not dominant brand colors.
- Typography: clear uppercase navigation and strong editorial display headings, with darker body text for readability.
- UI: compact sticky header, red primary CTA, white cards with subtle borders/shadows, structured service/pricing categories, and a clinical rather than floral visual language.
